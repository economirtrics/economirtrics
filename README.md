## Projects

**[Local labor-market effects of the 2025 U.S. tariffs](https://github.com/economirtrics/tariff-labor-research)** · R, Python
Rebuilt the county tariff-exposure measures from a Federal Reserve Bank of Richmond working
paper using HTS-10 customs records, reproduced its main regression, and estimated a monthly
event study of county unemployment. Tariff exposure overall has no detectable effect, and the
paper's significant result depends on separating two exposures that are 85% correlated.

**[Labor market elasticity dashboard](https://github.com/economirtrics/labor-elasticity-dashboard)** · Python, Streamlit
A live app that pulls FRED and BLS data on every load and estimates how employment responds
to wages and demand, by industry and state, over the full sample and on rolling windows.

**[Microprojects](https://github.com/economirtrics/microprojects)** · Python, Jupyter
Short data exercises: choropleth maps, chess Elo ratings, a simulation of the martingale
betting strategy, image steganography, and live National Weather Service forecasts.
